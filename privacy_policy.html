Privacy Policy

Clover Glow Privacy Policy

Clover Glow respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal information when you visit our salon, book or receive services, contact us, or use our website or booking tools. It applies to our activities in the United States.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

• Contact and account details, such as your name, email address, phone number, and booking information. • Appointment and service details, such as your appointment history, service preferences, and information you choose to share that is relevant to a service. • Payment information. Payments may be processed by third-party payment providers. We may receive transaction details, but do not receive or store full payment card numbers when payment is handled by those providers. • Communications and feedback, including messages, reviews, and responses to surveys. • Website and device information, such as IP address, browser type, and interactions with our website. Cookies or similar technologies may be used where available. • Photos or other images only when you provide them or give permission for them to be taken or used.

How We Use Information

We may use personal information to schedule and manage appointments, provide and personalize services, process payments, communicate with you, respond to requests, maintain business records, improve our services and website, protect against fraud or security issues, and meet legal obligations. We may also send promotional messages where permitted by law. You can opt out of marketing emails using the unsubscribe link or contact us to opt out of other promotional messages.

How We Disclose Information

We may disclose information to service providers who help us operate our business, such as booking, payment, communications, website hosting, and analytics providers. They may use the information only to provide services to us or as otherwise permitted by law. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a business transfer. We do not sell personal information for money. If we engage in targeted advertising or other data practices that qualify as a sale or sharing under applicable state privacy laws, we will provide any required notice and opt-out choices.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website or booking tools may use cookies and similar technologies to operate, understand usage, and improve the experience. You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some website or booking features.

Data Retention and Security

We keep personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including providing services, maintaining business records, resolving disputes, and meeting legal requirements. We use reasonable safeguards designed to protect information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information, and to opt out of certain processing, such as targeted advertising or the sale or sharing of personal information. You may also have the right to appeal a decision about a privacy request. To make a request, contact Clover Glow using the contact details provided through our salon or booking platform. We may need to verify your identity before responding. We will not unlawfully discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

Children’s Privacy

Our services and website are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information online from children under 13 without legally required authorization. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us so we can take appropriate steps.

Third-Party Services and Links

Our website or booking process may link to or use third-party services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, and we encourage you to review those policies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version and revise its effective date where appropriate. Your continued use of our services after an update means the revised policy applies to your subsequent interactions with us.

Contact Us

For questions or privacy requests, contact Clover Glow using the contact information provided by our salon or through the platform where you booked your appointment.
