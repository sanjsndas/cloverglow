A Guide to Maintaining Your Manicure Between Salon Visits

A fresh manicure can last longer with a little daily care. The right routine helps protect the polish, keep your nails comfortable, and make it easier to decide when it’s time for your next salon visit.

Protect your nails during everyday tasks

Nails are not tools, so avoid using them to open packages, scrape surfaces, or pry things apart. Use the appropriate tool instead. For chores involving water or cleaning products, wear gloves when practical. Frequent exposure to water and household cleaners can leave nails and surrounding skin feeling dry.

Keep hands and cuticles moisturized

Apply hand cream regularly, especially after washing your hands. Cuticle oil can help keep the skin around your nails soft; massage a small amount into the cuticles and nail edges as needed. Avoid cutting or picking at the cuticles, since this can irritate the surrounding skin.

Be gentle with polish

If you notice a small chip in regular polish, you can smooth the rough edge with a fine nail file and apply a thin touch-up layer if you have matching polish. Avoid picking at chipped polish or peeling off gel or other long-wear coatings. Picking can damage the surface of your natural nails. If a coating is lifting, ask your nail technician about safe removal rather than pulling it off.

Maintain a comfortable nail length

As your nails grow, file them gently in one direction to smooth snags and keep the shape even. Choose a length that suits your routine and feels comfortable. If a nail breaks, trim or file it rather than tearing it, which can worsen the break.

Follow the care advice for your manicure

Different nail services may need different aftercare. Follow the instructions your technician gives you, including guidance on water exposure, touch-ups, and removal. The right time to book another appointment depends on your nail growth, the service, and your preference; arrange a visit when the manicure no longer feels comfortable or looks the way you want.

If you notice persistent irritation, pain, or an unusual change around a nail, avoid covering the area and consider seeking advice from a qualified health professional. A gentle routine between salon visits can help keep your manicure looking neat while you care for your natural nails.
